Author’s Preface

There are moments in life that shake us to our core—moments that demand we pause, reflect, grieve, and ultimately, grow.

HOPE was born in one of those seasons. It is not just a collection of words. It is a sacred space where I laid down my fears, hopes, questions, and realisations.
